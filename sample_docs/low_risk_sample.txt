Advisory Call Transcript — Q3 Performance Review
Date: 2026-06-07
Participants: CFO, Head of Finance, External Auditor

---

CFO: Good morning everyone. Let's begin with the Q3 results. Revenue came in at $4.2 billion,
representing 5.3% growth year-over-year, which is comfortably within our guidance range of
4–6%.

Head of Finance: Correct. EBITDA margin held at 28.4%, a 40 basis-point improvement from the
same period last year, driven primarily by operational efficiencies in the cloud division and
reduced logistics costs in North America.

External Auditor: We have completed the independent review of all major revenue recognition
policies and found them to be fully compliant with IFRS 15. All transactions have been
properly documented and reconciled against supporting contracts and invoices.

CFO: Thank you. On the balance sheet side, we reduced net debt by $320 million through
disciplined working capital management. Our leverage ratio now sits at 1.8x EBITDA, well
below our covenant threshold of 3.5x.

Head of Finance: The board approved the dividend reinvestment plan at the annual general
meeting last month, with 62% of shareholders electing to reinvest. Share buybacks remain
within the approved programme limits under the existing shareholder resolution.

External Auditor: We are issuing an unqualified audit opinion for the quarter. All internal
controls over financial reporting are operating effectively. No material weaknesses or
significant deficiencies were identified during our review.

CFO: Excellent. For Q4 guidance, we are projecting revenue in the range of $4.4–4.6 billion,
assuming stable macro conditions. We will file the 10-Q with the SEC on schedule next week.

Head of Finance: The treasury team has also completed the annual LIBOR transition
documentation for all outstanding loan facilities, which are now benchmarked to SOFR.

External Auditor: No further issues to report. Everything appears to be in order. The audit
committee will receive the full management letter by end of week.

[END OF TRANSCRIPT]
